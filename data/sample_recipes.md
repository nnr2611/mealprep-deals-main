# Sample Recipes

## Lemon Chicken and Rice
Serves 4
- 600 g chicken thighs
- 300 g brown rice
- 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 300 g broccoli
Roast chicken at 200C for 30 minutes, cook rice, steam broccoli, combine.

## Lentil Tomato Soup
Serves 6
- 300 g lentils
- 800 g canned tomatoes
- 2 onion
- 200 g carrots
- 1 tbsp olive oil
Saute onion and carrots, add lentils and tomatoes with 1 L water, simmer 30 minutes.

## Overnight Oats
Serves 4
- 200 g rolled oats
- 400 g greek yogurt
- 300 ml milk
- 2 banana
Mix everything and refrigerate overnight.
